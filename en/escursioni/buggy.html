The experience

Off-road Sunset

Tenerife is a volcanic island: that means dirt tracks, lava trails and Martian landscapes. The right way to cross it is by buggy: two seats, a responsive engine and nothing but wind between you and the road.

The standard coastal route takes in El Puertito, Playa San Juan and the most scenic corners of the south. If you want to push further, there are off-road variants that tackle the more technical trails, plus the sunset option to finish the ride facing the sun going down over the Atlantic.

The activity starts with an introductory briefing: the vehicles are prepared for safe, engaging driving, and expert guides make all the difference between a "day trip" and a real experience.

The two options

🌅 Sunset Tour

Late-afternoon departure, coastal route with photo stops, arriving at the sunset spot over the ocean. Duration approx. 2h.

🌋 Off-Road

Technical tour across volcanic trails and dirt tracks. The adrenaline version — a category B driving licence is required. Duration approx. 2.5h.
