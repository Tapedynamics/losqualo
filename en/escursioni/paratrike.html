The experience

The paratrike combines the freedom of paragliding with an engine: more flexible take-offs and landings, longer flights and the ability to cover wide routes above Tenerife. A thrilling but fully controlled experience, perfect for anyone who wants to fly with the reassurance of a powered aircraft.

Tandem flight with a professional pilot. No experience required. 360° views over volcanoes, coastline and ocean, with the option of a custom route depending on the weather and your preferences.
