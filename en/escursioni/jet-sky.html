The experience

Feel the adrenaline of the Atlantic at full throttle with a jet ski experience made for anyone who wants to see the ocean from a completely different angle. Acceleration, freedom and spectacular views over the south Tenerife coast, in an activity that is intense, fun and safe.

Ideal for beginners and for those who have already tried water sports. Departure from the marina, then along the coast past cliffs, volcanic beaches and open water.
