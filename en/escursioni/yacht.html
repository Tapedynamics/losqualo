The experience

Midday, 12 guests, the open ocean ahead and nothing else around. A private yacht is the right way to experience Tenerife from the sea: no fixed itinerary — you decide where to stop, where to dive in, where to have lunch.

Several charter options are available — from the Fun Yacht for those who want comfort without going overboard, to the Luxury Yacht for a top-of-the-range experience, right through to the premium models. All come with a certified skipper, on-board catering and snorkelling gear included.

The options

Fun Yacht

12 pax · All inclusive · 3.5h / 5h / 7h

The only Fun Yacht in Tenerife. A private day out with water activities included — kayak, paddle surf, slide, water bike and aquascope — plus food, drinks and a swim/snorkel stop. Departure from Puerto Colón. Perfect for birthdays and stag or hen parties.

Luxury Yacht

12 pax · Premium · 4h / 7h

A high-end yacht with beautifully finished interiors and a professional crew. Premium cruising along the coast, a swim stop in a quiet spot and snorkelling gear on board. Ideal for special occasions — anniversaries, celebrations and VIP experiences.

The models

Armani

A private motorboat in a more intimate format — comfortable and versatile. Perfect for couples, small groups, sunset trips or an exclusive experience without a full-size yacht.

Shared sunset cruise

2h · sunset · €75/person

A shared trip to enjoy the sea, the sunset and the atmosphere of Tenerife without booking the whole boat. Includes open bar, a premium lunchbox, snorkelling gear and a professional captain. In summer: Tuesdays and Fridays at 19:00.
