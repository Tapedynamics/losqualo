The Vintage experience

We meet at the agreed point and the Momento begins there: an unhurried ride in the Vintage Car along the coast, all the way to the Sal Negra restaurant, right on the seafront.

After dinner, the return is included in the experience: same car, same calm, back to the meeting point.

The Vintage experience is available mainly in the area between Los Cristianos and Playa de Las Américas. The exact meeting and return point is confirmed after the booking has been checked.
