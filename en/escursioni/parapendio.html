The experience

A tandem paragliding flight alongside an expert pilot, to discover Tenerife from a unique perspective: cliffs, the volcanic cone of the Teide, the deep blue ocean and coastal villages seen from above.

No experience required. Take-off is from selected spots around the island, with smooth, scenic descents that are perfect for first-time flyers. Full briefing, harness and instructions before the flight.
