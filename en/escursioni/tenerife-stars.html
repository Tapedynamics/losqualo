The experience

A premium experience in the Teide National Park, built around three moments: first the Roques de García, then a transfer to a hand-picked viewpoint for sunset, and finally star observation under one of the purest night skies in Europe.

This isn't just an evening excursion — it's a carefully organised experience that brings together nature, food and astronomy in one unforgettable night at the Teide.

How it unfolds

🥾 1. Roques de García

  • Duration 45-60 minutes
  • Walk among the rock formations
  • The meal is served during this stage
  • Toilets and cafeteria available until 19:30

🌅 2. Sunset

Transfer to a scenic viewpoint to watch the sun set over the clouds, with the Teide behind you and the ocean on the horizon.

🔭 3. Star observation

Guided stargazing in one of only three skies in the world certified as a Starlight Destination for the quality of its night sky.
