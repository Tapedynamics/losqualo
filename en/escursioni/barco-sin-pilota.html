The experience

Want to take the helm yourself? With the licence-free boat you don't need any nautical licence: after a short safety briefing you head out on your own with your friends, choose where to stop and swim wherever you like. Departure from Puerto Colón (Costa Adeje), every day 10:00–18:00.

Two models available — the Idea 53, perfect for beginners, couples and relaxed coastal cruises, and the 15hp RIB dinghy (up to 4 people), easy to handle and great fun. Both come with fuel included, Bluetooth audio and swim stops along the coast.

Good to know

Rental by the hour or for the full day (up to 8 hours), every day 10:00–18:00. Refundable deposit at check-in · ID card or passport required. Besides the 15hp RIB (up to 4 people), the Idea 53 — licence-free and ideal for beginners — is available on request. Message us for up-to-date rates and availability.
