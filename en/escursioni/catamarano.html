The experience

The catamaran is the most stable, comfortable and photogenic way to spend a day at sea. Twin hulls, a large sundeck, a bow net to stretch out on and an indoor lounge for when you need shade.

Private catamaran for exclusive use, up to 22 people, departing from the Costa Adeje area: 3 hours of sailing past beaches and cliffs, responsible pilot whale and dolphin watching, plus a swim and snorkel stop in open water. On board you'll find a professional crew, aperitifs and drinks, and snorkelling gear. Choose from three included menus or external catering on request through our partners. Perfect for birthdays, stag and hen parties and private celebrations.

Classic itineraries

🐋 Whale watching

Pilot whale and dolphin watching in an area recognised as a "Whale Heritage Site".

🏝️ Adeje coves

Stop in sheltered bays, snorkel among volcanic rocks and have lunch at anchor.

🌅 Sunset Charter

Late-afternoon departure, aperitif under sail, sunset offshore and return to port.

🥂 Private events

Celebrate your special occasion on board: stag or hen party, birthday, anniversary or corporate event.
