The experience

A one-of-a-kind experience: you drive a real JetCar, a Lamborghini-inspired supercar, straight across the Atlantic Ocean. Speed, adrenaline and freedom come together in an exclusive activity that blends the thrill of a sports car with the energy of the sea.

Accelerate through the waves and feel like the star of a Fast & Furious film — the maritime edition. Departure from Puerto de Las Galletas – Pantalán nº 2, a quiet, well-connected spot in the south of Tenerife.
