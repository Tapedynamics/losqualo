The experience

Discover Tenerife in a genuine, thrilling way with a guided Jeep Wrangler experience, designed for anyone who wants to go beyond the usual routes and connect with the island at its purest.

A route of around three and a half hours through volcanic landscapes, scenic roads and hand-picked off-road tracks, combining nature, adventure and spectacular views.

Available routes

☀️ Teide By Day

Volcanoes, lava flows and high-mountain viewpoints.

🌅 Teide Sunset

Golden light, sunset over the ocean and a feeling like no other.

🌊 Coastal route

Contrasts between the sea, the cliffs and the villages of the south.

How it works

You can drive a Jeep yourself (up to 4 people per vehicle) or simply enjoy the ride as a passenger with a private driver. A flexible option, ideal both for adventurers and for anyone who'd rather sit back and take in the scenery.
