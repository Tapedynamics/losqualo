The experience

Nature, sea and adventure in their purest form. Discover the most authentic, wildest side of southern Tenerife with a kayak and snorkelling experience in one of the island's best-preserved stretches of coast: Playa La Arenita, in Palm-Mar.

A small natural refuge far from mass tourism, where the Atlantic meets untouched volcanic scenery. As you paddle you'll pass beneath the imposing Montaña de Guaza, an ancient volcano that shelters this coastline and creates a rich, surprising marine ecosystem. It's not unusual to come across sea turtles and colourful fish.

The details

⏱️ Duration

2 hours of guided adventure at sea: a gentle paddle along the coast, a stop to snorkel in open water and time to watch the marine life.

🧰 Included

Kayak and full equipment, snorkel mask and tube, neoprene wetsuit, dry bag, snack and water. Private tour option on request.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Who it's for

An activity suitable for everyone — families, couples, groups and children aged 3 to 12. No previous experience needed.

⚖️ Good to know

Maximum weight per kayak: 200 kg. Afterwards you can view and buy the professional photos taken during the tour.
