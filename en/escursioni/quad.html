The adventure

Live a one-of-a-kind volcanic adventure in the south of Tenerife on board a quad, crossing some of the island's most striking landscapes: from arid volcanic terrain to the forests of the Corona Forestal, with direct views of the Teide and, in the right conditions, the spectacular Mar de Nubes (sea of clouds).

The route takes you along scenic tracks and natural trails, where the raw power of the volcanic landscape meets the silence of the forest and the vastness of the ocean on the horizon. An intense, visual and authentic experience, designed to reveal the wildest side of Tenerife.

Included

🏍️ Quad and full equipment 🪖 Helmet 👨‍🏫 Professional guide 🛡️ Insurance 📸 Scenic stops at the viewpoints

Professional photos (optional)

Professional photos are taken at key points along the route, including viewpoints looking out over the Teide and the Mar de Nubes. At the end of the experience you can view and buy the images as a memory of your adventure.
