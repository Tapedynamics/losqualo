The experience

Step aboard a state-of-the-art catamaran and meet dolphins and pilot whales in the wild, sailing along the south coast of Tenerife — one of the areas in Europe with the largest population of resident cetaceans.

We sail respectfully and sustainably, following the official whale-watching protocols so you can enjoy marine wildlife in its natural habitat. Smooth, stable sailing, on-board commentary about the wildlife and the coastline, and plenty of time to relax and soak up the ocean.

What's included

🛥️ The fleet

Spacious, comfortable catamarans with shaded and sunny areas, comfy seating and, on some boats, hammock-style nets so you can lie just inches above the water.

🎒 Included

Return transfer from the covered areas in the south, food and drinks on board, professional crew, fuel, passenger insurance and live commentary.

🐋 Responsible watching

We sail according to the official protocols, respecting whales and dolphins so you can observe them without disturbing their natural behaviour.

🎯 Perfect for

Families, couples, groups of friends and travellers looking for an authentic, relaxing experience close to marine nature.

Boarding & pick-up

Departure point in the south of Tenerife (exact details shown on your voucher). Pick-up available from hotels and accommodation in the covered areas. Please arrive 15–20 minutes before departure. Several route options available, from short trips to longer, more complete excursions.
