About the property

Los Abrigos is a hostel-style property — a building of rooms — right in the centre of the village of Los Abrigos, one of the most authentic coastal spots in the south of Tenerife.

The property is designed for organised groups, tour operators, surfers, athletes and smart travellers, with a flexible yet well-run setup.

Room types

🛏️ Private rooms

  • Double rooms with shared bathroom
  • Deluxe double rooms
  • Double rooms with terrace
  • Twin rooms with single beds

⭐ Suite

Suite with balcony and private bathroom, ideal for coordinators, group leaders or premium guests.

🏠 Apartment

Self-contained apartment with a double bedroom, perfect for longer stays or for the group's key people.

🛌 Dormitory

Beds in a mixed dormitory with shared bathroom, to complete the group's capacity.

This makes Los Abrigos a hybrid stay + work solution, hugely appreciated by sports and creative groups.
