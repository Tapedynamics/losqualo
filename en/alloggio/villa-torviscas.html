Description

Torviscas Alto is one of the most convenient residential areas in the south of Tenerife: quiet, well connected, a short distance from beaches, water parks, shopping centres and a huge choice of restaurants and bars for every taste. This villa is the perfect base for families and groups who want to enjoy the liveliest side of Tenerife without giving up the calm of their own private space.

Generous indoor spaces, a garden with a barbecue area and a strategic location make this villa a practical, comfortable choice for a one- or two-week stay.
