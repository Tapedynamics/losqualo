About the house

The Surf House is a detached house in the south of Tenerife, in a particularly strategic spot for anyone who wants to surf, kitesurf or windsurf, or take part in surf camps and sports retreats.

Its main strength is how close it sits to several key areas, so activities can be adapted to the weather conditions and to the level of each participant.

Booking options

🛌 Hostel

Rent a bed in a shared room. Ideal for solo travellers on a budget.

🚪 Private room

Private double, triple, quadruple or 6-person room. Perfect for small groups.

🏠 Whole house

The entire house exclusively for groups and families. Maximum privacy and freedom.

Location

🪁 El Médano

Minutes from one of Europe's leading kitesurf and windsurf spots. Ideal when the wind is strong.

🏄 Calle México / Las Américas

The heart of Tenerife's urban surf scene. Surf schools, sandy beaches, perfect spots for beginners.

This location gives you real flexibility: strong wind → kite/wind at El Médano. Clean swell → surf at Las Américas.
