About the property

Bencomo Coliving is a coliving and coworking house in Santa Úrsula, in the north of Tenerife, designed for remote workers and digital nomads who want to work well and live the island as part of a small community.

Five rooms for up to 9 guests: private rooms and one shared room, a dedicated coworking space overlooking Teide and the sea, a pool, a sauna, a kitchen and common areas. The usual minimum stay is 14 nights.
