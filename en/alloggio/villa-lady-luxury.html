Description

Villa Lady Luxury is in Costa Adeje, the most exclusive area of southern Tenerife, and sleeps up to 6 people in 3 bedrooms with 3 bathrooms. Days revolve around the private pool, the garden and the terrace set up for lunch and dinner outdoors.

From the upper terraces the view runs from the Adeje mountains down to the sea: in the evening, with the pool lit up and the lights of the coast, the villa is at its best. Bright interiors, a large living room and a full kitchen to live in the house independently.
