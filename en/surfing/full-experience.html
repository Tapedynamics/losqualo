Full Experience

The tailor-made Lo Squalo package: accommodation + surf + activities + private services. We build the whole trip around you or your group. Surf sessions run with our partner school Ika Ika in Playa de Las Américas.

What the package includes

A bespoke package — accommodation, surf, activities and private services combined exactly the way you need them. Scalable from 5 to 14+ days.

🏠

Your own place to stay

From a bed in a hostel to a private villa with pool, depending on your group and budget. The entire Lo Squalo network is at your disposal (Luxury, Rural, Coliving, Hostel).

🏄

Ika Ika lessons

Qualified instructors, small groups (max 6 per instructor), board and wetsuit included. Beginner, intermediate or advanced coaching.

🌅

Activities

Sunsets on the Teide, jeep tours, paragliding, paratrike, catamaran, boat trips, diving, kayaking, morning yoga. We add whatever the group wants to live.

🤵

Private services

Airport and daily transfers, grocery delivery, private chef, BBQ at the villa, extra cleaning, babysitting, event decor. All coordinated by Lo Squalo.
