IKA IKA

The School

Ika Ika Surf School was born out of pure love for the ocean and for surfing. Based in P.° Guadalajara, in Playa de las Americas, just 50 metres from the sand, it is the ideal starting point whether you are catching your first wave or sharpening your technique.

Lessons run from complete beginner to advanced, equipment is available for rent, and our staff speaks Italian, Spanish, English, French and German. After your session, a chill-out lounge, lockers and private showers are waiting for you.
