SURF BAR

The Concept

The LO SQUALO Surf Bar Franchise is a repeatable model for launching, repositioning and turning a profit on a bar or small restaurant with a genuine surf identity — without starting from scratch. It is not a classic franchise. It is not a rigid format. It is a real activation system, built on hands-on experience in venues, events, day-to-day operations and applied marketing.

How We Work

🏠 The venue is always yours

Your venue, your investment, your business. LO SQUALO comes in as an operating and brand partner, never as owner. The property and the responsibility stay with you.

📋 The contract is always yours

Every agreement with landlords, suppliers or licensing bodies is signed by you. Nothing passes through LO SQUALO. Transparency is the foundation of the relationship.

🏄 LO SQUALO brings brand, method and support

Visual identity, operating system, marketing strategy and an active presence during launch. Everything you need so you never have to improvise.

🤝 A clear, professional, no-grey-areas relationship

Roles defined from day one. No surprises, no hidden costs, no unnecessary dependencies. A partnership run with judgement.

Who It's For

📈 The right fit

  • People who want to open or relaunch a venue with a real plan
  • Anyone looking for a recognisable format that adapts to their space
  • Anyone who prefers a proven method to improvising from zero
  • Operators who already have a venue but need identity and a system

🚫 Not for you if

  • You want a standard franchise contract with fixed royalties
  • You do not want to take direct responsibility for the business
  • You are not willing to work actively in the venue
  • Your project has no minimum budget to launch properly
