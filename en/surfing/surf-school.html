Overview

Set in the heart of Playa de Las Americas, this iconic surf school is the place to experience surfing in Tenerife the authentic way: safe, real and genuinely fun. Lessons are built for every level.

From your very first contact with the water to polishing advanced manoeuvres, the Lo Squalo team guides every student through a progressive, clear method adapted to their needs. The goal is not just to learn to surf: it is to feel a real connection with the ocean.

Why Choose This Surf School

👥 Small groups

Maximum 6 students per instructor. Personal attention guaranteed in every session, with genuine tracking of individual progress.

🏁 Expert instructors

A certified, multilingual team (ES, IT, EN, DE) with years of experience in Tenerife's waves and full water-rescue training.

🏄 All the gear

Foam boards and shortboards, wetsuit, leash and insurance included in every lesson. You don't need to bring a thing.

📈 A progressive method

A structured learning system in phases: theory on the sand, balance drills, first waves and refinement. Safe, clear and effective.

🌊 The real thing

Learn where the locals actually surf. A school with soul, not a tourist attraction. You can feel the surf culture in every session.

Types Of Experience

👥

Group Lessons

Up to 6 people. The most popular option. You learn alongside others, keep the motivation high and live surfing as a community.

🏄

Private 1:1 Lessons

Maximum attention, fastest progress. Ideal if you want results in a short time or have specific goals.

👪

Semi-Private Lessons

2 to 3 people. The perfect balance between price and attention. Ideal for couples or friends.

📅

Packs And Programmes

Packs of 5 or 10 sessions at a special rate. For anyone ready to commit to surfing seriously.

☕

Equipment Rental

Boards, wetsuits and accessories available for independent surfers. By the hour or by the day.
