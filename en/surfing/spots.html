Surf Spots in South Tenerife

South Tenerife is one of the island's most popular surf zones: a huge variety of waves along the coast, for beginners and seasoned surfers alike. Below is our working map, zone by zone — the same one we use every day to take you where the waves are good today.

Playa de Las Américas

The surf epicentre of southern Tenerife. Here surfing is part of the identity of the place: schools, surf shops, bars, an international crowd and waves for every level. Several important peaks sit within it.

La Fitenia

Las Américas · next to the pier

A spot right by the pier, in front of the San Borondón restaurant. It works mainly in summer with a south, south-west or south-east swell. Rarely too crowded.

Rights and lefts Summer Beginner / Intermediate S – SW – SE
Lo Squalo Tip Relaxed vibe — a great place to hang out after the session too.

Yefrys

Playa de Las Américas

A less crowded wave, best suited to advanced surfers. A long right that demands good wave reading and a quick exit off the peak. It fires in winter but can also work in summer.

Long right Winter (summer too) Advanced Volcanic bottom
Lo Squalo Tip Respect the volcanic bottom: you need to read the wave well and get off the peak fast.

El Dedo

Playa de Las Américas

A winter wave, very local and strictly for high-level surfers. It breaks straight onto the rocks and the current can be strong.

Winter wave Advanced Very local Strong current
Lo Squalo Tip Only paddle out with a guide or a local contact.

Piscinas

Playa de Las Américas

A good spot to start surfing, especially on small days. When the swell picks up, it gets considerably more demanding.

Small conditions Beginner Progression
Lo Squalo Tip Excellent for your first sessions on small days; far more technical once it grows.

La Derecha

Playa de Las Américas

A powerful, technical right-hander. A spot deeply respected by the local community.

Right-hander Powerful and technical Advanced Respected by locals
Lo Squalo Tip Not recommended without the right level or someone from the area with you.

El Medio

Playa de Las Américas

One of the most popular spots in Playa de Las Américas, and where a lot of surf lessons take place. The bay has a fairly even rocky bottom, with a mellower area for beginners and more technical peaks for advanced surfers.

Beach / reef All levels Lots of schools Technical peaks
Lo Squalo Tip Friendly zone if you are starting out, with sharper peaks just a bit further along for the advanced crew.

The Spanish Left

Playa de Las Américas

A legendary, well-known wave: powerful and technical. For experienced surfers only, and heavily respected by the locals.

Legendary left Powerful and technical Experts Highly respected
Lo Squalo Tip For most visitors, this one is best enjoyed from the promenade.

Other zones on the south coast

Beyond Las Américas, the south coast has a spot for every condition: from sheltered back-up options to the wind kingdom of El Médano.

Playa de Las Galletas

South-east · near El Médano

A very local spot. Access and reading the line-up are not always straightforward. It can deliver good waves far from the crowd.

Very local Away from the crowd Tricky access
Lo Squalo Tip Best visited with a local contact.

Playa de El Médano

East coast · Granadilla

Best known for windsurfing and kitesurfing thanks to the steady wind, but under the right conditions it also serves up interesting waves. It includes areas such as La Machacona, La Tejita and El Cabezo.

Windsurf / Kitesurf Steady wind Waves in the right conditions
Lo Squalo Tip Watch out for wind, currents and sharing the water with wind/kite riders.

Playa de La Tejita

Next to the Montaña Roja

Set in a protected natural landscape beside the Montaña Roja. Waves over a sandy bottom and a far wilder setting.

Beach break Sandy bottom Natural setting
Lo Squalo Tip Perfect if you want surf and scenery in the same session.

Playa de Alcalá

Western side of the south coast

On the western side of southern Tenerife, with quality waves and fewer people. Some of the spots here are very local and very technical.

Quality waves Less crowded Local, technical spots
Lo Squalo Tip Without the level or a local contact, better to watch these from the promenade.
